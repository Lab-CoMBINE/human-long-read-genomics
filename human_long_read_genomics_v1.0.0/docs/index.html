About

About the Explorer

A read-only interactive companion to the review “The long-read era of human genomics” by Marta Baragli and Alberto Magi, Department of Information Engineering, University of Florence.

Scope

Computational tools and quantitative benchmark results accompanying the human long-read genomics review.

Benchmark philosophy

The Explorer reports results under the benchmark conditions used in the study. It does not rank tools outside those conditions.

Data

All plots are generated from the TSV tables distributed with this release.

Version

Release v1.0.0. Static, read-only benchmark and tool companion.
